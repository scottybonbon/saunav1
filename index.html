A sauna is the rare room that slows a group down. Phones stay outside. The talk gets easier, or it stops, and nobody minds. It is the most human room you can add to a home, and the one people remember.

Four simple things, older than language, and still the fastest way back to your own breath.

Wood

Cedar that warms the air and gives off a scent you feel before you can name it.

Water

A ladle across the stones. The room softens, and so do you.

Stone

Mass enough to hold the heat even and kind, never sharp.

Heat

The oldest way we know to put the day down and come back to yourself.
